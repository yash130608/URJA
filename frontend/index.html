What your numbers mean

kWh (units)

One kilowatt-hour is the energy used by a 1,000-watt appliance running for one hour.

Estimated bill

Your estimated kWh multiplied by your selected per-unit rate. Your actual bill can include other charges.

Estimated CO₂

A broad illustration using an approximate grid-emissions factor, not an official carbon report.
